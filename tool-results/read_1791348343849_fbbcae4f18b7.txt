   234→          <h3>READING THE INK</h3>
   235→          <p><b>Red flashes</b> can be <b>deflected</b>. Deflects fill the attacker's <b>posture</b> — the amber bar under their health. A full bar <b>breaks</b> them: a broken foe glows gold, bears a pulsing 死, and your next cut is a one-strike <b>DEATHBLOW</b>. On bosses, a deathblow carves a chunk of their life. Posture bleeds away if you stop pressing — stay aggressive.</p>
   236→          <p><b>危 — perilous.</b> Attacks marked with the red kanji <i>cannot be deflected</i> and hit 40% harder. Dash through them, or leave. A last-instant dodge is a <b>perfect dodge</b> (見切): it restores Ki and sharpens your next cuts.</p>
   237→          <p><b>Gold arcs</b> from bosses are their proudest cuts — deflect those for heavy posture damage. <b>Red line-dashes</b> are perilous: dodge only.</p>
   238→          <p>Melee yokai attack in chains. Keep swinging, keep deflecting. You take no contact damage — only telegraphed attacks hurt, and every death is readable in hindsight.</p>
   239→          <h3>THE STYLE RANKS</h3>
   240→          <p>Hit without being hit and the rank climbs: 初 → 中 → 上 → 極 → 鬼 → 神. Higher ranks pay more ash. Get hit and the rank bleeds.</p>
   241→          <h3>A BESTIARY OF SMALL HORRORS</h3>
