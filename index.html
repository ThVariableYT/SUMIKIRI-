READING THE INK

Red flashes are attacks about to land — dodge, dash through, or parry them. Golden flashes belong to bosses; a golden cut can only be answered with a parry.

You take no contact damage — only telegraphed attacks hurt. Every death is readable in hindsight.

THE STYLE RANKS

Hit without being hit and the rank climbs: 初 → 中 → 上 → 極 → 鬼 → 神. Higher ranks pay more ash. Get hit and the rank bleeds.

A BESTIARY OF SMALL HORRORS

Kappa Imp act I — the fodder. Hunts in packs.
Oni Brute act I — slow, heavy slams. Circle it.
Tengu Scribe act I — keeps its distance, throws fans. Slice the fans out of the air, or parry them back.
Borei Wraith act II — fast lunges along a red line.
Inugami Hound act II — charges and detonates. Kill it early and it dies quietly.
Kame Guard act III — shielded from the front. Flank it, or break the shell with the Kanabō.
Chōchin Broker act III — heals its allies. Silence it first.
Gōma Sorcerer act IV — summons endlessly and teleports when cornered.
Ashen elites act IV+ — ringed in red. Faster, harder, worth triple.
